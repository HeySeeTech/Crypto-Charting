# Subject-specific evidence for Chinese X commentary

Read for market-event posts. Select the subject below; do not scan every institution or asset unless requested. Use current primary sources, then independent reporting to test competing explanations. Keep raw data and research separate from public copy.

## Crypto asset

Start with the asset's price and actual trading, spot demand/supply and derivatives, chain activity, supply changes, and protocol/governance developments. Prioritize what plausibly explains this event; record unavailable or stale material rather than padding the post. BTC mining/issuance and settlement differ from ETH staking/burn or an ecosystem token's unlock schedule. ETF flows are one channel; DEX activity is not token-holder revenue by default. Identify active versus forced trading only with evidence. Check external policy, macro and geopolitics when their route to this asset is material.

## Fed / FOMC

Use official statement, implementation note, press conference, minutes and projections appropriate to their actual release dates. A meeting need not include new projections. Compare the decision and language with verifiable pre-event expectations, not only the prior rate. Separate no rate change from an easier expected future path. Link to funding costs, yields, dollar or risk appetite only as observed or conditional. Never promote a reference author's hike forecast into consensus.

## SEC / CFTC

Read the actual release, filing, order, rule, docket or court document. Identify agency jurisdiction, affected products and participants, procedural stage, effective date and conditions. An application or acknowledgment is not approval; withdrawal of one case is not blanket legalization. Explain what permission, obligation, access or legal cost actually changes. No compulsory bullish or bearish conclusion.

## Treasury / OCC

For Treasury distinguish auctions, settlement, cash balance, debt management, sanctions and fiscal law. Announced issuance is not automatically a measured liquidity drain; state accounts, dates, offsets and counterparties needed for that claim. For OCC read the actual interpretive letter, charter or supervisory action: eligible banks, permitted services and remaining conditions. Permission to provide custody does not establish bank purchases of crypto.

## ETF / ETP issuer

Separate issuer announcement, regulatory filing, listing approval, first trading day, assets, holdings, shares, subscriptions/redemptions and secondary-market turnover. Check product domicile and whether spot, futures, leveraged or multi-asset. Compare holdings/flows on aligned dates; price-driven AUM growth is not inflow. Fee waivers and capacity do not establish demand. Explain what the issuer action changes for access, cost or actual buying.

## Macro release

Record reference month/quarter, release timestamp/timezone, revisions, seasonality and unit (monthly/annual/annualized). Obtain a dated forecast before describing a surprise. Separate headline/core and volatile components. A higher CPI does not mechanically determine core PCE; revised past data can alter interpretation. Explain whether the release changes the expected policy path, real income, demand or corporate costs, and distinguish observed market reaction from a forecast.

## Company / earnings / cross-market context

Use issuer IR and filings to verify dates and operating results. Separate earnings growth, prior guidance, consensus and market response. Include the company only when it supports the chosen reader question; do not claim a tech earnings result mechanically determines crypto. For company-led posts retain the company as protagonist.

## Elections / geopolitics as connected risks

Distinguish an election date from result finality, legislative control from enacted law, proposed talks from an implemented ceasefire, and proxies from direct state combat. Verify energy/transport disruption rather than assuming it. A possible route is supply disruption → energy prices → inflation/rate expectations → holding costs/risk appetite; mark unobserved links as scenarios. Check diplomacy and contrary market moves too. Do not turn personnel evacuation into a confirmed war countdown.

## Linking evidence without writing a list

Useful structures include a price move meeting several successive catalysts; two markets giving different answers to the same story; a policy benefit delayed while a cost is immediate; or a new release invalidating the reason behind an earlier move. Let events and participant behavior determine paragraph order. Do not reuse a past week's particular Fed/PCE/earnings sequence as a standing calendar.

## Delivery and persistence

For this user, keep the public post about the subject's actual business/economics, technical development, ecosystem and verified events. Do not invent investor intentions or lead readers toward trading. Interpretation and conditions for revising an assessment should concern the subject, not when readers should buy, sell or stake. No meaningful new development or explanation means no forced post; an unsolicited tutorial does not fill that gap.

Research note briefly states delta, question/connections, judgment/evidence and change-of-view condition. Public post must stand alone and use natural spoken Chinese; source details and as-of limitations follow it. Never force a recommendation when no useful new fact or explanation exists. Honor workspace storage and archive rules; absent a specified destination, save generated posts and evidence inside the active workspace outputs/ or research/ with dated folders. Do not publish, send or sync externally without the corresponding authorization.
