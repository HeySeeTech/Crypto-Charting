VolatilePair Overview

Understand VolatilePair, a Uniswap v4 hook that sets a separate LP fee for each swap direction on pools of volatile pairs.

VolatilePair is a Uniswap v4 dynamic fee hook for volatile pairs such as ETH/USDC. On every swap, the hook uses a custom algorithm to set swap fees. Both the ask and bid fees are determined by the algorithm.

There are four design goals accomplished by the dynamic fee:

  1. Constant spread in calm blocks. The pool aims to quote a fixed spread.
  2. LPs share backrun value. When a large trade moves the price, fees in the backrun direction are raised to capture a share of backrun value.
  3. Lower LVR. The side that informed flow trades on pays more. The total spread stays the same.
  4. Less sandwiching. The mid-block extra makes the back leg of a sandwich pay more.

Only Uniswap Labs can create VolatilePair pools. Uniswap Labs creates each pool and sets its fee parameters in the same transaction. See Pool Creation and Configuration.
