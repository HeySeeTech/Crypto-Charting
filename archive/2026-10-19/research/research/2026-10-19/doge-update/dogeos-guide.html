Thank you for testing the DogeOS Chikyū Testnet. If you have questions or want to give feedback, join our Discord !

The Testnet consists of Dogecoin’s Testnet and the DogeOS Testnet test network.

To view L1 transactions, check out the Dogecoin Testnet Explorer . To view L2 transactions, you can use DogeOS L2Scan .

Here is the suggested workflow to explore the Testnet:

  1. Add the Testnet  configurations to your wallet.
  2. Request DOGE on the DogeOS Testnet network from our Faucet .
  3. Transfer DOGE (or other tokens) to any wallet on DogeOS Testnet using your wallet.
  4. Explore our ecosystem and interact with our featured dApps. (Coming soon!)

You can find the instructions for each app in the rest of this user guide.

Questions & Feedback#

If you encounter any issues, join our Discord  and talk to us in the #general channel. We would love to hear your thoughts or feedback on how we can improve your experience, too.
