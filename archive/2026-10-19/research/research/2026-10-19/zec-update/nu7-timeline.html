ebfull September 17, 2026, 10:42pm 1

NU7 is proceeding!

After the recent sentiment collection results (which clarified sentiment collection from earlier this year), discussions among the various orgs (ZF/Tachyon/Valar/ZODL/SL), feedback from proponents of proposals, and a discussion between Zakura and Zebra teams about timelines, there is now unanimous agreement among the orgs and engineering teams throughout the Zcash ecosystem.

  • Testnet activation: October 6, 2026
  • Mainnet activation: November 5, 2026

The main features included in this upgrade will be 25-second block spacing (ZIP-218), disabling v4 transactions, and NSM integration (ZIP-234 (alt), ZIP-235) following the poll results: preserving halvings, with reintroduction starting February 2031.

New transaction formats do not appear in this network upgrade! The changes in this upgrade should not affect wallets in any significant way. Infrastructure such as full nodes, indexers and block explorers may be affected.

Checklist

I’ll use this thread as a checklist to keep people aware of what’s going on.

  • Code completion. September 30, 2026 - Features should be implemented and ready for inclusion in a testnet upgrade.
  • Testnet activation heights decided. Zakura and Zebra teams agreed at R&D Meeting to height 4465026 on Testnet.
  • Testnet activation. October 6, 2026 - The official Zcash testnet will upgrade to NU7.
  • Final mainnet activation decision. October 20, 2026 - The activation height and decision for mainnet activation, given experience with NU7 on testnet, will be set at this point. (This takes place a week before Zcon.)
  • Mainnet activation. November 5, 2026 - NU7 goes live on mainnet.
