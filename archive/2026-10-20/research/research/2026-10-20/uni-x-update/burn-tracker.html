Burn History

Note: this chart, like the “Latest Day” / 7D / 30D cards and the “Burn by Chain” panel above, is booked on the day each burn happened. That is the amount each chain's burn address actually recorded that day, Total Burned and “Burn Composition” accumulate the on-chain balance of the mainnet burn address (0x…dEaD) transaction by transaction, so they include the 100M one-time treasury burn that landed on 2025-12-28, in the same window as the fee switch. A cross-chain burn takes 1–7 more days to settle to mainnet after it is recorded on its own chain, which means these two figures are the same UNI booked at two different moments, and they must not be added together.
