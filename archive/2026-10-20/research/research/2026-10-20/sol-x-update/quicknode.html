Update

**What to expect during the Alpenglow switch (Oct 21)**

- Timing. Anza estimates the activation at about 14:50 UTC. At the current block rate it could come as early as 13:20 UTC. The switch-over follows about 20 minutes later. Please be ready from 13:00 UTC.

- For a few seconds while the network hands over to Alpenglow, new transactions won’t be included in blocks. If the network takes longer to complete the switch, the pause lasts until it does.

- Around the switch, sendTransaction may briefly return a “node unhealthy” preflight error. Retry, or send with skipPreflight.

- Subscriptions at processed commitment may show a few slots that are never confirmed. Use confirmed or finalized for anything that must not be reverted.

- Yellowstone gRPC streams may disconnect once around the switch. Clients with automatic reconnect need no action.

- After the switch, confirmed and finalized stay within about a slot of each other, so finality is much faster.

Scheduled

Solana Mainnet is switching to Alpenglow, Solana's new consensus protocol (SIMD-0326). The Solana developers at Anza plan to turn it on Wednesday, October 21, around 14:50 UTC.

**What this means for you**
- This change applies to Solana Mainnet. Testnet and Devnet already run Alpenglow and are not affected.
- The network may be briefly unstable while the switch-over happens.
- Your Quicknode Solana endpoints stay the same. You don't need to change anything.

**What we're doing**
- Our Solana Mainnet infrastructure already runs Agave v4.3.0.
- We're pausing all planned maintenance on Solana Mainnet during the upgrade window.
- Our team will actively monitor the network during and after the switch-over, and we'll post updates here if we see any impact.

**Try Alpenglow now**
If you want to check how your application behaves under Alpenglow before it reaches Mainnet, it's already live on Solana Testnet and Devnet.
