      "The risk appetite of investors contracted in the past few weeks fueled by the worrying combination of AI funding and AI model safety; escalating US-Iran war activity; anxiousness in front of US midterm elections and generally rising bond yields around the world. This has triggered a selloff in AI stocks including semis and memory stocks, and has further led to selloffs in the Korean and Taiwan markets, both of which are dominated by semis and memory stocks," stated Thomas "Tom" Lee, Chairman of Bitmine.

      "Crypto is widely held in the US, Korea and Taiwan. So naturally, when investors in those regions de-risk, this creates spillover pressures in crypto markets. Bitmine's ETH holdings are 4.9% of the ETH supply (of 122.1 million ETH).

      "We believe Bitmine is positioned to outperform the crypto bull market that we expect to be coming, as the company has strengthened its competitive position over the past year in the midst of a crypto winter (bear market)," continued Lee.

      "Over the past week, we acquired 12,535 ETH. Bitmine's track record of consistent buying of crypto is unmatched by any public company in the world. Bitmine has bought ETH each and every week since the inception of the ETH Treasury Strategy on June 30, 2025," stated Lee.

While MAVAN was originally developed to support Bitmine's own Ethereum treasury, MAVAN has expanded to serve institutional investors, custodians, and ecosystem partners seeking best-in-class staking infrastructure. A portion of Bitmine's ETH is already staked on the MAVAN platform.

      As of October 18, 2026, Bitmine total staked ETH stands at 5,067,309 ($12 billion at $2,365 per ETH). "Bitmine has staked more ETH than other entities in the world. At scale (assuming Bitmine's ETH is fully staked by MAVAN and its staking partners), the projected ETH staking reward would be $369 million on an annualized basis (using 2.58% 7-day BMNR yield). This compares to annualized staking revenues at $309 million based upon 5.1 million staked ETH (84% of the 6.04 million ETH held by Bitmine)," continued Lee.
